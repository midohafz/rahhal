السجلات والخدمات الخارجية

الروابط الخارجية والعمولة

يحتوي الموقع على روابط إلى مواقع فنادق وشركات طيران وسياحة. بعض الأزرار روابط شراكة مع Booking.com ومع شبكات شراكة مثل CJ (Commission Junction): إذا حجزت عبرها قد نحصل على عمولة دون أي تكلفة إضافية عليك. ولا يتأثر اختيارنا للفنادق بذلك المقابل. وقد تضع هذه الشبكات ملفات ارتباط (كوكيز) على متصفحك بعد النقر لتتبع الحجز ونسبته إلينا. عند الضغط على رابط تنتقل إلى موقع آخر له سياسة خصوصية وكوكيز خاصة به، ويتم الحجز والدفع هناك وليس عندنا، ولسنا طرفاً في العقد بينك وبينه.

الشريط الصغير

يعرض الموقع شريطاً صغيراً في الأسفل لإعلامك بروابط الشراكة. عند الضغط على «فهمت» نحفظ هذا الاختيار في متصفحك فقط (localStorage) حتى لا يظهر الشريط مرة أخرى، ولا يصل هذا الاختيار إلينا.

The images are stored within the site itself and are not loaded from external services. The site keeps copies of its pages on your device so they work offline (the app cache), and you can clear them from your browser settings. If we add analytics or ads, we will update this page and ask for your consent where required.

The site links to hotel, airline and tour company websites. Some buttons are affiliate links with Booking.com and with affiliate networks such as CJ (Commission Junction): if you book through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Our choice of hotels is not affected by this. These networks may set cookies in your browser after you click, to track the booking and credit it to us. When you click a link you leave this site for another with its own privacy and cookie policy. Booking and payment happen there, not with us, and we are not a party to your contract with that company.

Accuracy of information

The information is general and may change, including prices, schedules, and hotel names and management. Check with the provider before any booking. We do not guarantee that it is complete or accurate.

Responsibility

We are not a travel agency or a tourism service provider, and we are not responsible for third-party services, their quality, or cancellations.

The notice bar

The site shows a small bar at the bottom to inform you about partner links. When you press “Got it”, we store that choice only in your browser (localStorage) so the bar does not reappear. This choice is not sent to us.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete any message you sent or ask about your data by emailing the address above.
