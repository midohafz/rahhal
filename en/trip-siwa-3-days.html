Siwa Oasis in 3 days

Cleopatra Spring, the salt lakes, the Mountain of the Dead and a desert sunset.

3 daysBest for: Quiet and the desert

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Times are approximate. Check site hours, ticket rules and transport schedules before you travel, as they may change.

Day 1 — Arrival

  • Morning: Arrive in Siwa. The journey is long, so check the transport option and its duration.
  • Afternoon: Cleopatra Spring: a warm spring among palm trees.
  • Evening: Dinner in town.

Day 2 — The desert

  • Morning: The salt lakes, for floating. Bring fresh water to rinse off the salt.
  • Afternoon: The Mountain of the Dead: painted tombs and a view over the palms.
  • Evening: Gabal Dakrur and a desert safari at sunset with a licensed driver.

Day 3 — Return

  • Morning: The town market.
  • Afternoon: Return journey.

Tips for this itinerary

  • Visit the spring early in the morning when it is calmer.
  • Carry water and a hat, as the desert is hot by day.
