Frequently asked questions

How many days do I need in Cairo?

Plan at least two to three days: one for the Pyramids, one for the Grand Egyptian Museum and one for old Cairo (Khan El-Khalili and the Saladin Citadel).

Should I stay in Giza or downtown Cairo?

A hotel in Giza saves travel time to the Pyramids, while Nile hotels near Tahrir Square or Garden City suit downtown Cairo and the Egyptian Museum better.

When should I visit the Pyramids?

Go early, before the heat and the crowds.

Should I book tickets in advance?

We recommend booking Grand Egyptian Museum tickets ahead. At every historic site ask for an official ticket and do not pay anyone who does not give you one.
