We do not sell flight tickets. These are the airlines’ official websites for checking flights and fares.

EgyptAir

The national carrier, with the widest domestic network.

Routes: Cairo, Sharm El Sheikh, Hurghada, Marsa Alam, Alexandria, Luxor, El Alamein, Aswan, Abu Simbel

Official website

Air Cairo

A low-cost airline owned by EgyptAir, running domestic and regional flights.

Routes: Cairo, Sharm El Sheikh, Hurghada, Marsa Alam, Luxor, Aswan, Abu Simbel

Official website

Nile Air

A private airline focused on domestic flights between tourist cities.

Routes: Cairo, Sharm El Sheikh, Hurghada, Luxor, Aswan

Official website

Nesma Airlines

An Egyptian airline owned by Saudi Nesma Group, with domestic and regional flights.
