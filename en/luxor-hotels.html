Frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for Luxor?

Two days cover the main sights: one for the East Bank (Karnak and Luxor Temple) and one for the West Bank (Valley of the Kings and Hatshepsut Temple).

What is the best time to visit Karnak?

Sunset is calmer and prettier.

When should I visit the Valley of the Kings?

Start early in the morning, and you can add Hatshepsut Temple the same day.

Where should I stay in Luxor?

The hotels in this guide are beside Luxor Temple, on the East Bank or on a Nile island, so choose by how close you want to be to Karnak and Luxor Temple. Names and management of some historic hotels change, so confirm at booking time.
